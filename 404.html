MediaBay comes from a simple idea: marketing should move. The journey began with Mediabike, a mobile advertising concept created in 2013 to bring brands physically into the streets.

Over time, the same instinct expanded into websites, e-commerce, social communication, SEO, AI and digital products. MediaBay is the evolution of that experience.
