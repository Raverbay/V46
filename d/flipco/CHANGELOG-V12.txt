FLIP&CO V12 — CONTEXTUAL HEADER

Changed from V11:
- Homepage header is invisible at initial load without waiting for JS.
- While scrolling, only the centered logo appears, floating transparently over the page.
- When scrolling stops, the full navigation expands into a subtle frosted-glass header.
- When scrolling resumes, the navigation collapses back to the logo-only state.
- Returning to the top hides the header again.
- Internal pages keep the full header immediately available.
- Reduced visual weight: no pill/background around the logo-only state.
