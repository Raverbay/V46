THE MEDIA BAY VIEW / STRATEGY & BRAND

KNOW
WHAT YOU
STAND FOR.

A brand is not only a logo, a colour palette or a nice-looking website. It is the system of decisions behind what a business says, shows and builds.

We start by understanding the business: what it sells, who it serves, what makes it different, where it wants to go and what is currently getting in the way.

Then we turn that understanding into a direction that can actually be used — by the website, by social, by campaigns, by sales and by every future touchpoint.

CLARITY
BEFORE
OUTPUT.

The point of strategy is not to make the project slower. It is to stop the business from producing the wrong things faster. A clear direction gives every creative decision a reason.

01

POSITIONING

Where should the business sit in the mind of the customer? We clarify the offer, audience, category, differentiation and territory the brand can credibly own.

02

BRAND
IDENTITY

We translate the direction into a visual language: logo direction, typography, composition, graphic elements and practical rules that make the brand recognisable.

03

MESSAGING

We define what needs to be said and how it should be said — claims, tone of voice, hierarchy, key messages and the language customers encounter along the journey.

04

ART
DIRECTION

We establish the visual direction for campaigns, website, social, commerce and physical communication so different outputs still feel like the same brand.

HOW WE WORK

FROM
QUESTION

There is no fixed formula for every business. The process adapts to the starting point, but the logic stays the same: understand first, define second, create with purpose.

01

DISCOVER

Business, audience, offer, competitors, context and ambitions. We identify what is true, what is unclear and where the opportunity sits.

02

POSITION

We define the strategic territory: who the brand is for, what it promises, what makes it relevant and how it should be perceived.

03

Strategy becomes language and identity. We establish the messages, tone, visual direction and rules that make the brand coherent.

04

ACTIVATE

The system moves into the real world: website, social, campaigns, e-commerce, advertising and physical touchpoints.
